Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Overview

This privacy policy explains how we handle information in connection with our simple messaging service using Twilio. We message only a small number of people we know personally or who have explicitly agreed to receive messages from us.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or retain any personal data from recipients. The only information used is the phone number provided for the purpose of sending a message. We do not keep records of messages, phone numbers, or any other data after the message is sent.

We do not use cookies, tracking, analytics, or any data collection tools on this page or in our messaging process.

How We Use Information

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver the intended message via Twilio. We do not store phone numbers, message content, or any related data on our systems.

Data Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell, rent, or share any information with anyone. Twilio processes the messages solely for delivery as required by their service.

Consent

We only send messages to people who have given clear prior consent (for example, by asking to receive a message or agreeing in advance). Messages are limited to a handful of known contacts.

Data Retention

We retain no data. Phone numbers and message details are not stored by us.

Your Rights

You can reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. If you have questions, contact us at the information below.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us directly. This is a personal/small-scale messaging use and not a commercial service.

Note: This policy is provided for compliance with messaging platform requirements (such as Twilio). We prioritize privacy by design and collect nothing.
